Balcão Pizza Napoletana

A neighbourhood pizzeria that wanted to sell direct, without delivery-app commissions. The site became the place's digital counter.

See the live site ↗balcaopizza.com.br

01The challenge

Balcão makes Neapolitan pizza one at a time, with dough fermented for 48 hours, for pickup at the counter on Alameda Augusto Stellfeld.

The customer is the neighbour. The place wanted to take orders directly, without delivery-app commissions, and for that, ordering on the site had to be as easy as ordering in an app.

On the other side of the counter, the team needed to see orders arriving in order, without having to scroll through the WhatsApp chat.

02Design decisions, and the reason for each

  1. A menu in feed mode.

    Pizza is chosen by the photo. On mobile, the menu becomes a feed: one pizza per screen, swiping up, double-tap to like. The likes make up the “most loved” list.

  2. Orders straight to WhatsApp.

    No app in the middle and no commission. The site puts the message together with the pizzas, the notes (“no onion”) and the pickup time, and the customer just hits send. Payment still happens at pickup: Pix, card or cash.

  3. The spinning pizza, as a signature.

    When customers reach the menu, the pizza spins down onto the plate; when they choose another, it changes. It's the detail that makes the site memorable, and it stays still for anyone who asks for less motion.

  4. Open or closed, live.

    The site knows the opening hours, Tuesday to Sunday, 6 pm to 11 pm, and shows at the top whether Balcão is open right now. Nobody sends an order to a closed kitchen.

  5. The day's dough calls the shots.

    48-hour dough can't be improvised. The team enters in the dashboard how many dough balls they have; each pizza ordered on the site takes one off, so do counter sales, and at zero the site stops taking orders.

03What was built

For customers

  • Menu with notes on every pizza
  • Calculator: how many pizzas for your group
  • Loyalty card: 10 orders, 1 free pizza, with stamps on your phone
  • Link to track the order, from oven to pickup
  • “Suggest a flavour”, straight to the restaurant's WhatsApp
  • Reorder the last order

For the restaurant

  • Password-protected dashboard: orders in columns, with a sound alert
  • Stages: received, dough stretched, in the oven, ready, picked up
  • Loyalty card stamps and customers
  • The day's dough and “sold 1 at the counter”
  • Big screen with the orders for the shop floor
  • Review request after pickup

Behind the scenes

  • Server that recalculates the total of every order
  • Order limit per connection, against abuse
  • Online payment ready, switched off until the restaurant decides
  • Flyer with a QR code for the site and WhatsApp
