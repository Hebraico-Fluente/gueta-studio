Pétala & Lucas

A wedding website that looks like the couple, not a template with their names swapped in.

See the live site ↗petalaelucas.guetastudio.com.br

01The challenge

Pétala and Lucas have been legally married since 2022 and decided that 2027 would be the year to celebrate with the people they love most.

They already had an identity: a logo, hand-drawn lettering and their own colours. Off-the-shelf wedding websites swap the name and the photo, but everything else is the same for everyone, and many keep a cut of the gift money.

The site had to carry that identity forward and answer guests' questions before they turned into WhatsApp messages to the couple.

02Design decisions, and the reason for each

  1. Their identity, not a theme.

    The couple's logo, the hand-drawn lettering and the coral and green colours are on every screen. Guests recognise the wedding before reading a single word.

  2. RSVP by name, for the whole invitation.

    Guests type their name as it appears on the invitation and reply for everyone on that invitation, in a minute. Nobody retypes the whole family, and the couple know who has already replied.

  3. Gifts via Pix, straight to the couple.

    No commission. The list looks like them, with gift contributions written by the couple themselves, and each gift opens the Pix QR code.

  4. Details that only make sense for them.

    The “How well do you know the couple?” quiz, with a ranking among friends, the timeline since 2017, which draws itself as you scroll, and a secret: five taps on the heart in the logo play the Djavan song.

  5. Answers before the questions.

    In “Ask”, guests type their question and the site answers only with what the couple have already shared there. Anything still unanswered stays with them.

03What was built

Before the party

  • Countdown and “Save to calendar”
  • Our story, with documentary mode
  • Couple's quiz, with a friends' ranking
  • Ask: questions answered with what the couple have published

The big day

  • Where and when, with an illustrated map
  • The party schedule
  • Dress code, explained in three cards
  • Message wall

RSVP and gifts

  • RSVP by name, per invitation
  • Gift list with photos and Pix by QR code, commission-free
  • Animations switched off for anyone who prefers less motion
