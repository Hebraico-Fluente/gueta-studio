Draft Interiores

A high-end joinery whose site looked like a template, rebuilt with the look of a furniture studio.

See the live site ↗hebraico-fluente.github.io/draft-interiores

01The challenge

Draft designs, builds in its own workshop and installs kitchens, bedrooms, walk-in wardrobes and wall units.

The work is high-end, but the old site looked like a template and didn't show it.

People looking for custom joinery want to see real work, understand the process and know who they'll be talking to.

02Design decisions, and the reason for each

  1. “Turn on the light.”

    The LED lighting is designed together with the furniture, and it's what changes the room at night. A switch at the top turns the light in the photo off and on, and a whole section shows the same pieces with the planned lighting.

  2. A portfolio of real projects only.

    In joinery, the proof is the photo. Projects can be filtered by room, and each one opens with the photos, the materials and the colours used. Shortcuts at the top go straight to kitchens, walk-in wardrobes, TV wall units and bathrooms.

  3. The founder up front.

    What sets Draft apart is that you talk to the person who makes it, with no middleman. That's why Marcos appears on the very first screen, with an invitation to talk to him directly.

  4. A quote that asks first.

    Four quick questions: rooms, stage of the build, name and city, timeline. The message reaches Marcos's WhatsApp ready to go, and the first reply is already on target. Nothing is stored on the site.
