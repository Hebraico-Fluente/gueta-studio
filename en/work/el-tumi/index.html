El Tumi Café

A specialty café with a Peruvian soul and a rare brewing method, the Mupeco, explained in five seconds.

See the live site ↗moshikgueta.github.io/El-tumi

01The challenge

El Tumi serves specialty coffee brewed in the Mupeco, a ceramic cup sitting on a clay pot, and Peruvian flavours such as salteña, alfajor and mazamorra morada.

Few people around here know the Mupeco, and it's what makes the café different. The site needed to explain the method without a wall of text.

On top of that, the menu is long, and the café welcomes people who speak Portuguese, Spanish and English.

02Design decisions, and the reason for each

  1. A Mupeco you brew by dragging.

    A method is best understood by doing it. Visitors drag and watch, in four stages, the water cover the grounds and the coffee pass through the ceramic into the cup. Alongside, an illustrative comparison with a paper filter.

  2. Two ways to read the same menu.

    Feed mode is for discovering by photo, one item per screen. The full list, with 55 items, favourites, vegan and gluten-free filters and a search, is for those who already know what they want.

  3. Three languages, one tap.

    The café has a Peruvian soul, and its counter welcomes people who speak Portuguese, Spanish and English. The switch sits at the top and works instantly, without reloading. Every sentence has its own version in the site's own dictionary, with no machine translator involved; reviews stay in their original language, because they are quotes.

  4. Praise only with a source.

    The reviews come from customers on Google, with their photos, and every press quote links to the original article: Mural do Paraná, Bem Paraná and the Kafex guide.

03What was built

The café

  • Highlights in photos of the café, which enlarge with a tap
  • The interactive Mupeco
  • Live open or closed status, at the top
  • Visit: address and opening hours

The menu

  • 55 items, in categories
  • Feed mode with likes
  • Filters: favourites, vegan, gluten-free
  • Search by name

The house

  • Tumi shop: Café Tumi 250 g, the Mupeco and a box of alfajores, for pickup at the café
  • Events calendar, with a button to add events to your phone's calendar
  • Reviews and press
  • Portuguese, Spanish and English
