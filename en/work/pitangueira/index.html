Pitangueira

The price list became a quote the couple builds on their own, with the brand's humour.

See the live site ↗pitangueiracriativa.com.br

01The challenge

Pitangueira, run by Pétala and Isa, creates visual identities and stationery for weddings: from the save the date to the favours.

That's 38 items in six categories, each with its own price. In a table, it is hard for the couple to read and hard to answer for whoever handles the enquiries.

The brand has a lot of humour, and the site needed to speak the same way. And every order had to land somewhere organised, not get lost among the WhatsApp chats.

02Design decisions, and the reason for each

  1. A quote instead of a table.

    The couple picks the items, sees the total instantly and understands what they're getting: each item has a short line explaining what it is. Items charged per unit, such as illustrations, get a quantity field; anything without a fixed price shows as “to be agreed”.

  2. The order goes out ready on WhatsApp.

    Conversations with couples already happen on WhatsApp. The site simply puts the message together with the items and the total, and nobody has to copy anything from a table.

  3. The partners as stickers.

    Pétala and Isa are the face of the brand. At the top, they appear as cut-outs with a burgundy outline, as in their Instagram posts, and come in with a slight spin. On mobile they are smaller, in the corners, away from the text. With “reduce motion” on, they simply appear.

  4. A leads board, not a spreadsheet.

    Every quote sent through the site becomes a card: New, In conversation, Proposal sent, Closed or Lost. Couples who arrive via Instagram or by referral are added by hand to the same board, and the partners' phones get an alert for every new order.

03What was built

The site

  • Quote with 38 items in six categories, from invitations to artwork applied to objects
  • Instant total and a ready-made order on WhatsApp
  • About us, with Pétala and Isa
  • Portfolio: stories they have already designed
  • Instagram videos in a cinema section
  • Frequently asked questions

The dashboard

  • Password-protected access
  • Orders in columns, from New to Closed
  • Notes on each couple
  • Search, filters and monthly numbers
  • New leads added by hand
  • Phone alert for every order

Behind the scenes

  • Site and dashboard on Cloudflare, with their own database
  • Prices, categories, portfolio and FAQs in one easy-to-edit block
  • Automatic publishing with every change
