Questions

Before we start.

Do I need to understand technology?

No. You send me the content, the photos and what makes your business special. I take care of everything else, including putting the site live.

What if I change prices or the menu?

With maintenance, I update it for you (up to four requests a month). Without maintenance, we agree on each update separately.

How long does a site take?

On average 10 to 15 working days, counted from when I receive your material. The first preview arrives within seven days. Platforms have their own schedule.

How much does a website cost?

It depends on each project: what your business needs, the pages and the features. First I prepare a no-obligation preview and, with it, a tailored quote.

How does payment work?

50% when the project is approved and 50% on delivery, by Pix or bank transfer.

Am I tied to you?

No. The domain and files are in your business's name. Maintenance is optional and can be cancelled with 30 days' notice.

Do you work outside Curitiba?

Yes. Our first call can be by video, in Portuguese, Spanish, English, Italian or Hebrew, and the site works the same in any city or country.
