01For the guests

Everything they'd ask you on WhatsApp, answered on the site.

The site stays with your guests from the invitation until after the party. Each part can be switched on or off to suit your wedding.

Beforefrom the invitation to the eve

Before the party

  • Your identityThe couple's logo, colours and lettering on every screen, not a theme shared with other couples.
  • Countdown and “Save to calendar”One tap and the date goes into Google Calendar, iPhone or Outlook.
  • Our storyYour timeline, which can also be watched as a documentary, one chapter per screen.
  • Personal invitation and RSVPEach family gets its own link and replies for the whole invitation: events, menu and plus-ones. Anyone without the link can RSVP by name on the site.
  • Gifts via PixA list with photos and a Pix QR code straight to your account, commission-free. Guests tap “I've sent a gift” and you find out.
  • Couple's quiz and your songQuestions about the two of you, with a friends' ranking, and the couple's song in the Spotify player.

Duringthe big day

On the big day

  • Where, when and how to get thereA map, the party schedule, the dress code explained and the most common questions already answered.
  • Everyone's tableWhen you release it, each family's invitation shows which table they will sit at.
  • Message wallGuests write, and a message only appears on the wall once you approve it.
  • Guests' photos on the big screenEveryone sends their party photos from their phone. The ones you approve go into the gallery and play on the big screen, along with the messages.

Afterto keep

After “I do”

  • All the party photos in one placeThe gallery stays on the site, with the photos your guests sent and you approved.
  • Time capsuleMessages with a photo or audio that stay sealed until your 1st, 5th or 10th wedding anniversary. Not even you can read them before then.
  • Guests' privacyEach guest's personal data is used only to organise the wedding and is deleted within the period you choose.

06How it works

Five steps, no fuss.

  1. A chat

    On WhatsApp: the date, your style and what the site needs to have.

  2. Identity

    With Pitangueira, or based on the logo and colours you already have.

  3. Site goes live

    You review and approve it. Then you edit whatever you like from the dashboard.

  4. Invitations and replies

    Each family gets its own link, and the replies arrive in the dashboard, by event.

  5. After the party

    Photos, messages and the time capsule stay on the site. Guests' data is deleted within the chosen period.

07Questions

Before you say yes.

Can't find your question? Ask on WhatsApp. I reply within a day.

How much does it cost?

It depends on what you want on the site and what the wedding is like. Send a WhatsApp message with the date and what you have in mind, and I'll explain everything.

Does the gift money go through the site?

No. Guests pay with your Pix, by QR code or with the copy-and-paste code, and the money lands straight in your account, commission-free. Anyone who taps “I've sent a gift” shows up in the dashboard, so you can check it against your bank statement.

Do guests need to download an app or create an account?

No. The site opens in the browser on a phone or computer. To RSVP, all they need is the invitation link or their name as it appears on the list.

Can we change things once the site is live?

Yes. From the dashboard, you edit the site section by section and can switch parts of it on or off.

What happens to our guests' data?

Only you can see it, and it is used only to organise the wedding. It is deleted within the period you choose, 30 to 365 days after the wedding, and you can export everything beforehand.

We already have a visual identity. Can we use it?

Yes. The site starts from your logo, colours and lettering, as with Pétala & Lucas's. If you don't have one yet, Pitangueira creates it.

Do you work outside Curitiba?

Yes. We can talk on WhatsApp or by video, and the site works the same in any city.
